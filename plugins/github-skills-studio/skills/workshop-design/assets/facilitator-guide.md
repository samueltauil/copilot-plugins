## Stuck-learner protocol

1. Ask what they see and what they expected.
2. Look at Mona's last comment together.
3. Apply the troubleshooting table.
4. After five minutes, move them on and follow up asynchronously.

## Closing

- Recap capabilities, not completion counts.
- Share the survey and the follow-up exercise.
- Remind learners that exercises stay available to finish on their own.

## After the session

- Collect feedback into one tracking issue per exercise.
- Turn recurring confusion into content fixes; re-run walkthrough tests.
